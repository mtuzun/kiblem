TITLE (max 30):
Qibla & Prayer Times Guide

SHORT (max 80):
Prayer times, qibla, azan reminders, Quran, hadith and dhikr counter.

FULL:
Qibla & Prayer Times Guide is an all-in-one companion designed for your daily worship.

Prayer Times
See the Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha times for your location or any city you choose. All times appear side by side at a glance, and a live countdown shows the time left until the next prayer. The Gregorian and Hijri dates are on the home screen. When you open the app, if you have granted location permission and you are online, your location is found in the background. If your city has changed, you are asked whether to update the prayer times.

A Separate Alert for Every Prayer
Set a separate alert for Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha. For each prayer, choose how long before the prayer you want to be alerted (on time, or from 1 to 60 minutes) and pick the alert sound. You can use one of your phone's ringtones or your own audio file (for example an mp3). You can receive the alert as a normal notification, or on Android as a full-screen alarm that opens even on the lock screen.

Qibla Compass
Use your phone's sensors to find the direction to the Kaaba from where you are. When you face the right direction, you get instant visual confirmation. The map view also shows the qibla direction on a map.

Verses of the Quran and Audio Listening
Read verses of the Quran with the Arabic text, transliteration, the Diyanet (Presidency of Religious Affairs) and Elmalili Hamdi Yazir Turkish translations, and an English translation. Browse verse by verse and surah by surah through the whole Quran with the forward and back buttons, and share the verse you like. For audio, you can choose to hear only the Quran recitation, only the translation, or both one after the other. You can choose the Diyanet, Elmalili or English translation; the translation is read aloud by your phone's text-to-speech. If you like, you can download the Quran text once and read it offline too.

Hadith of the Prayer Time
A different short hadith is shown for each prayer time and changes every day. The hadiths are compiled from sources such as Bukhari, Muslim, Tirmidhi, Abu Dawud and Ibn Majah, and the source is shown under each hadith.

Dhikr Counter
Keep track of your count digitally while praying with prayer beads, and reset it whenever you like.

Themes and Appearance
Choose between the Classic, Leather, Ocean and Night themes. Change the color of the home screen and figures such as a mosque, crescent and star, Kaaba, Seljuk star, lantern and tulip. Pick the light, dark or system theme and adjust the text size. The app is available in Turkish and English.

Home Screen Widget
Add the widget to your Android home screen to see the next prayer time and the time remaining without opening the app.

Note: The Turkish and English versions of the hadith texts are free translations prepared for this app. We recommend consulting the original sources for details.
