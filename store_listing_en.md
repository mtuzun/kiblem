Prayer times, azan reminders, qibla compass, Quran, hadith and a dhikr counter: an all-in-one companion for your daily worship.

Prayer Times
See the Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha times for your location or any city you choose. All times appear side by side at a glance, and a live countdown shows the time left until the next prayer. The Gregorian and Hijri dates are on the home screen. When you open the app, if you have granted location permission and you are online, your location is found in the background. If your city has changed, you are asked whether to update the prayer times.

A Separate Alert for Every Prayer
Set a separate alert for Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha. For each prayer you can choose how long before or after the prayer you want to be alerted (on time, or from 1 to 60 minutes) and pick the alert sound. Choose one of the 8 built-in melodies, a ringtone of your phone, or your own audio file (for example an mp3). You can receive the alert as a normal notification, or on Android as a full-screen alarm that opens even on the lock screen.

Qibla Compass
Use your phone's sensors to find the direction to the Kaaba from where you are. When you face the right direction, you get instant visual confirmation. The map view also shows the qibla direction on a map.

Verses of the Quran and Audio Listening
Read verses of the Quran with the Arabic text, transliteration, the Diyanet (Presidency of Religious Affairs) and Elmalili Hamdi Yazir Turkish translations, and an English translation. Browse verse by verse and surah by surah through the whole Quran, and share the verse you like. For audio, you can choose to hear only the Quran recitation, only the translation, or both one after the other. The translation is read aloud by your phone's text-to-speech. If you like, you can download the Quran text once and read it offline too.

Hadith of the Prayer Time
A different short hadith is shown for each prayer time and changes every day. The hadiths are compiled from sources such as Bukhari, Muslim, Tirmidhi, Abu Dawud and Ibn Majah, and the source is shown under each hadith.

Dhikr Counter
Keep track of your count digitally while praying with prayer beads, and reset it whenever you like. The counter can also be opened on the lock screen, where the volume buttons count for you, even while the phone is in your pocket.

3-Day Weather
See the weather for the next three days with the lowest and highest temperatures, and a short description such as rainy, foggy or sunny under each icon. It refreshes by itself and also shows the last saved data offline. Weather data comes from MET Norway.

Themes and Appearance
Choose between 8 themes: Classic, Leather, Ocean, Night, Sunset, Black, Sky and Easy Read. In the Sky theme, clouds drift slowly across the top of the screen. Easy Read is made for older users and small screens, with a high-contrast black background, a very large countdown and big prayer time rows. Change the color of the home screen and figures such as a mosque, crescent and star, Kaaba, Seljuk star, lantern and tulip. Pick the light, dark or system theme and adjust the text size. The app is available in Turkish and English.

Home Screen Widget
Add the widget to your Android home screen to see the next prayer time, the time remaining, today's weather and the time without opening the app. You can adjust the widget's color and size, and make its background transparent.

Note: The Turkish and English versions of the hadith texts are free translations prepared for this app. We recommend consulting the original sources for details.
